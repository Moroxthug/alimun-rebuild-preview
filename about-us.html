Our story

Built by language teachers who got tired of watching great students stall out in expensive, infrequent 1:1 lessons. Alimun pairs daily live practice with AI feedback so progress happens between classes too, not just during them.

Start today

A Standard for Real Fluency

We built Alimun because we wanted to offer a new way to learn a language: real preparation with a teacher, at a price more people can afford.

Your teacher leads every class and plans what you learn. Between classes, AI gives you instant feedback on your essays and tests and helps your teacher grade your homework, so your teacher’s time goes into teaching and you keep practising at your own pace.

Learn English live

Classes are open in English and German. Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, Mandarin, Japanese and Arabic have a free waitlist, and we email you as soon as your language opens.
