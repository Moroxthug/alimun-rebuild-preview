LET'S LEARN TOGETHER.
FOR LESS.

Alimun is a live online language school. You learn in a small group with a teacher, at a fixed time each week, and practise between classes with notes and AI feedback.

  • 01

    Find your level. A free level test places you from beginner (A1) to advanced (C2), and you join a class at that level.

  • 02

    Learn live. Each class is a 60-minute video session with your teacher and the same group, on the same days and time every week.

  • 03

    Keep the class with you. After every session you get the session notes and the homework your teacher sets.

  • 04

    Practise between classes. Essays, homework and tests are graded by AI the moment you submit, with exercises for your level and a progress page that shows what to practise next.

Classes are open in English and German. Want another language? Get an email when it opens.

FaQs

Can’t find the answer you’re looking for? Please chat to our friendly team or write us an email at info@alimun.com

  • Which languages can I learn?

    Classes are open in English and German. Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Mandarin, Japanese and Arabic are coming: join the waitlist and we will email you when your language opens.

  • How much do the classes cost?

    Plans start at €24 a month: Community €24, Focused €59, Intensive €109 and 1 on 1 €269 a month. Yearly plans cost the same as 10 months. Prices are shown in your currency.

  • How do the classes work?

    Each class is a live 60-minute video session with your teacher and your group, on the same days and time every week. Depending on your plan you meet 1, 2 or 3 times a week.

  • What level do I need?

    Any level. A free level test places you from beginner (A1) to advanced (C2), and classes are grouped by level.

  • What happens between classes?

    You get the notes of every session and homework with AI feedback, so you keep practising until the next class.

  • How can your classes be so affordable compared to private tutors?

    One teacher teaches a small group instead of a single person, so the cost of the class is shared. That is how a live class with a teacher starts at €24 a month.
