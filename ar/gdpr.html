GDPR Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Kleinefy Solutions Inc., doing business as Kleinefy Inc. and Alimun ("Alimun", "we", "us" or "our"), collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use alimun.com, our dashboards, live classes and related services (the "Service"), and the rights you have over it.

It is written to meet the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Italy's Personal Data Protection Code, Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Quebec's Law 25, US state privacy laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), Australia's Privacy Act 1988 and Morocco's Law No. 09-08. Section 11 adds details for specific countries and regions.

1. Who We Are

The controller of your personal information is Kleinefy Solutions Inc., [COPY NEEDED: registered office address], Canada. Our Privacy Officer, who is also the person in charge of the protection of personal information under Quebec law, can be reached at privacy@alimun.com.

When a company buys classes for its employees, the company decides what progress information it receives, and we process that information on its behalf as described in our agreement with it.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the Service:

  • Account and profile: name, email address, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), role, the language you learn or teach, your level and language preferences.
  • Learning data: placement test results, class enrolments and schedules, attendance, homework and essays, quiz and test answers, scores, teacher and AI feedback, flashcards and practice activity.
  • Live classes: your name, video, audio and chat as transmitted to other participants during a class. Alimun does not record live classes. Speaking exercises may use your browser's built-in speech recognition, which is provided by your browser maker under its own privacy terms.
  • Teacher applications: CV, credentials, specialty, biography, location, years of experience, introduction video and, for payouts, payment and tax details.
  • Payments: plan, billing period, transaction history and subscription status. Card numbers are entered on Stripe's pages and processed by Stripe; we do not store them.
  • Communications: messages you send through the contact form or by email, newsletter and waitlist sign-ups, and support conversations.
  • Technical and usage data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, dates and times, error logs and, with your consent, analytics and marketing cookie data, as described in our Cookie Policy.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive data such as health, religion or ethnic origin. Please do not include it in homework, messages or classes.

3. How We Use It and Our Legal Bases

  • To provide the Service (create your account, place you in classes, run live sessions, deliver homework, feedback and practice, take payments and handle support): performance of our contract with you.
  • To produce AI-assisted feedback and exercises: performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep the Service secure (prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms, fix errors) and to improve it (measure usage, develop features and curriculum using aggregated or de-identified data): our legitimate interests, balanced against your rights.
  • To send newsletters, waitlist updates and marketing, and to use analytics and marketing cookies: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep accounting and tax records, respond to lawful requests and meet legal obligations: compliance with law.

Service emails about your account, classes, payments and changes to our terms are part of the Service and are not marketing.

4. AI Processing

To generate exercises and draft feedback, we send the relevant text (for example the exercise or your homework, your level and language) to AI providers acting as our processors: Google (Gemini), Anthropic and Groq. We do not sell this content, and we instruct our providers not to use it to train their models where their services allow it. [COPY NEEDED: confirm that the AI keys used in production are on paid plans whose terms exclude training on customer content.]

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you. Homework grades drafted by AI are reviewed and sent by your teacher, and automated practice scores do not affect your account, billing or access. You may ask a person to review any automated result by contacting us.

5. Who We Share It With

We never sell your personal information or rent it out. We share it only as needed to run the Service, with:

  • Your class: your teacher sees your profile, work and progress; other learners in a live class see your name and anything you share in the session.
  • Your employer, if it pays for your classes: attendance, level and progress, as agreed with it.
  • Service providers acting on our instructions: Supabase (database, authentication and storage, hosted in the EU), Vercel (website and server hosting), Stripe (payments), Daily.co (live video), Resend (email), Google, Anthropic and Groq (AI features), Google Analytics (analytics, with consent) and Wise or other payout providers (teacher payouts).
  • Authorities and advisers where required by law, to protect rights, safety and property, or to our professional advisers under confidentiality.
  • A buyer or successor in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, bound by this Privacy Policy.

6. International Transfers

We are based in Canada and our providers operate in Canada, the European Union, the United States and other countries. Our main database is hosted in the EU (Ireland). When personal information leaves your country, we protect it with adequacy decisions (the EU recognises Canada as adequate for commercial organisations subject to PIPEDA), the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and UK Addendum, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified, and contractual commitments that require comparable protection. Information transferred abroad may be accessible to the courts and authorities of those countries. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above:

  • Account, profile and learning data: while your account is open, and deleted or anonymised within 90 days after you close it.
  • Payment and invoice records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, generally 6 to 10 years depending on the country.
  • Teacher applications that are not accepted: up to 12 months.
  • Contact form messages and support emails: up to 24 months after the conversation ends.
  • Newsletter and waitlist sign-ups: until you unsubscribe or the waitlist closes.
  • Server logs: up to 12 months. Cookie data: as stated in our Cookie Policy.

We may keep information longer where needed to resolve disputes, enforce our agreements or comply with a legal obligation.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls with row-level security, role-based permissions for teachers and staff, hashed passwords and reputable hosting providers. No system is completely secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the competent authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Learners under 18, or under the age of digital consent where they live (16 in some EU countries, 14 in Italy), may use the Service only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information without that consent, contact privacy@alimun.com and we will delete it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • delete your information, or close your account;
  • restrict or object to our processing, including for direct marketing;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out;
  • ask for human review of automated decisions;
  • complain to a data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@alimun.com. We will verify your identity and respond within one month (extendable where the law allows), or sooner where local law requires. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. You can update most profile data and close your account from your dashboard.

United Kingdom

You may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Our UK representative under Article 27 UK GDPR is [COPY NEEDED: UK representative name and address].

Canada and Quebec

We handle personal information in line with PIPEDA and applicable provincial law, and obtain your consent for collection, use and disclosure except where the law allows otherwise. You may withdraw consent subject to legal and contractual restrictions. You may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca). If you live in Quebec, you also have the rights under Law 25, including to be informed of automated decisions and to have information de-indexed, and you may complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca). Our person in charge of the protection of personal information is our Privacy Officer, reachable at privacy@alimun.com.

United States

In the last 12 months we have collected the categories of information described in Section 2: identifiers, customer records, commercial information, internet and network activity, audio and video during live classes, professional information for teachers, and inferences about your language level. We use and disclose them for the business purposes in Sections 3 and 5. We do not sell personal information, and we do not knowingly sell or share the information of anyone under 16. If we use marketing cookies that count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising, you can opt out at any time through the Manage Cookies link in the footer, and we honour Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out. Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states with privacy laws may exercise the rights in Section 10, use an authorised agent, and appeal a refusal by replying to our decision. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit.

Australia

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Some providers who receive your information are located outside Australia, including in the United States, the European Union and Canada; we take reasonable steps to ensure they handle it in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. You may complain to us first and, if you are not satisfied, to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Morocco

Your personal data is processed in accordance with Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data. You have the rights of access, rectification and objection under that law, and you may complain to the Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel (CNDP, cndp.ma). 12. Marketing Emails

We send newsletters and promotional emails only with your consent or where the law otherwise allows it, in line with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), the US CAN-SPAM Act, the EU ePrivacy rules and Australia's Spam Act. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also unsubscribe by emailing privacy@alimun.com.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the new version with a new date and, for material changes, notify you by email or in your dashboard before they take effect.
